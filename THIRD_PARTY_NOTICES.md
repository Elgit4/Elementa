# Third-party artwork

## Open Peeps

- **Creator:** Pablo Stanley
- **Collection:** [Open Peeps](https://www.openpeeps.com/)
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
- **License statement:** The official Open Peeps website states that the collection is free for commercial and personal use under CC0.
- **Source files used:** `public/illustrations/open-peeps/peep-14.svg`, `peep-66.svg`, and `peep-89.svg`.
- **Source mirror for the ready-made SVG templates:** [Open Peeps Studio](https://github.com/Hantuhan/peepstudio), which identifies its templates as Open Peeps by Pablo Stanley under CC0.

These assets are included as local SVG files so the app does not depend on a third-party image host at runtime. The SVG artwork is not generated by Elementa.
